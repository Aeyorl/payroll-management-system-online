Individual Tax Preparation

Accurate individual tax preparation and timely income tax filing is essential for any effective and efficient tax plan. Our understanding of "audit-triggers" and the importance of the presentation of tax information allows us to minimize potential audit engagements. We take the time to look for and recommend tax savings opportunities for every client.

We prepare all forms of individual federal and state tax returns. We can prepare returns for all states that have a state income tax. We have extensive knowledge working with most all tax issues including, but not limited to, interest/dividends, small business Schedule C, farming Schedule F, Capital gains and losses Schedule D, rental properties/royalties, and K-1 income/losses Schedule E. We also have experience reporting retirement benefits, ACA (health care) information and installment sales.

Our tax professional assists individuals who are dealing with IRS issues get them resolved quickly and efficiently.
