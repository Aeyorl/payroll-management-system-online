About Us

Crystal Tax Inc. was started in 2011 by Yasser S. Hamed and it has become one of the leaders for tax preparations in the area.


Over our existence, we have prepared a variety of tax returns which contain personal taxes, business taxes and its implications. We have also helped a lot of troubled client situations through Federal, State and Local levels.


We are willing to challenge any unfair tax judgments.


Our mission is your satisfaction. Our core principles and number one policy is to provide exceptional customer service to our individual and business clients.


We, not only offer our services, but our advice is also available to you. A few of our services include: Corporation Taxes and Formation/Dissolution, Partner (multi member LLC), bookkeeping system for new and existing businesses and more.
