Individual Tax Preparation

Accurate individual tax preparation and timely income tax filing is essential for any effective and efficient tax plan. Our understanding of "audit-triggers" and the importance of the presentation of tax information allows us to minimize potential audit engagements. We take the time to look for and recommend tax savings opportunities for every client.

We prepare all forms of individual federal and state tax returns. We can prepare returns for all states that have a state income tax. We have extensive knowledge working with most all tax issues including, but not limited to, interest/dividends, small business Schedule C, farming Schedule F, Capital gains and losses Schedule D, rental properties/royalties, and K-1 income/losses Schedule E. We also have experience reporting retirement benefits, ACA (health care) information and installment sales.

Our tax professional assists individuals who are dealing with IRS issues get them resolved quickly and efficiently.

Key advantages of utilizing an experienced, trained, educated tax professional at Payroll Management System Online will provide you with:

PERSONALIZED SUPPORT

At Payroll Management System Online, we are able to look at the whole picture and determine the best alternatives for certain tax positions. We are able to explain all tax positions and provide necessary planning for future tax services.

DUE DILIGENCE

We do our due diligence to ensure that your tax return is accurately prepared and properly filed. If your tax return is selected for examination, you will have the reassurance that your return was properly prepared and filed.

EXPERIENCE

We have extensive experience working with most all tax issues facing taxpayers today.

REDUCED AUDIT RISK

We have years of experience in government auditing. We know how to look for "red flags" that may trigger an IRS examination thereby helping our clients avoid costly and aggravating IRS inquiries and examinations.

COMPETITIVE PRICING

Our fees are competitive with other tax preparation services. We are significantly less expensive than most CPA firms with fees typically less than the commercial and franchise tax preparation services. We charge by the forms used and offer discounts to new and current clients.

TIME SAVINGS

According to the IRS, it takes the average taxpayer around 22 hours to finish a form 1040, 10 hours to complete a form 1040A and 7 hours to complete a form 1040EZ. All of those hours can be saved and used for something more important to you by allowing us to prepare your tax returns.
